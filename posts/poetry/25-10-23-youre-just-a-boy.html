You're just a boy

Posted: 23/10/2025

Maybe love has something to do with accepting someone for who they are, in totality. It explains why people can love another and never wish to see them again. It explains peoples' confidence in others and themselves. But there's probably more to it than that. Like the rule of law in LAWS206; defining it is useless, but understanding it is important, and it can occasionally be glimpsed if you look at it with an unfocused eye. Just don't stare at it for too long or you'll blind yourself.

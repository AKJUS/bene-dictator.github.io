I'll break promises today, to go on

Posted: 05/11/2025

Something I enjoy about writing poetry is finding out the meanings I didn't consciously intend. Especially when I realise what I'm writing about reflects my feelings for more than one individual at once, and for people who I feel oppositely about. It can become quite interesting, since the poem becomes doubly alive, like a twin that ended up estranged to the other but were once close.

I enjoy finding these dichotomies, they seem to permeate my life in opposing truths. It feels like life is one big attempt at reconciling things as best you can.

Photos taken at the Rockwood Conservation Area in Ontario, Canada, on the 4th of January 2025 and the 20th of December 2024 respectively.

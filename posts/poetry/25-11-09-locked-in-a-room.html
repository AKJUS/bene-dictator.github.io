So many different things I don't want to say.

I recently read something about known knowns - what we do know we know; known unknowns - what we know we don't know; and then unknown unknowns - what we don't know we don't know. The last is the scariest, because you can't prepare for what you can't anticipate. It may also be the most rewarding.

A question to that little bit was, "what about unknown knowns? Things we don't know we know." The curt answer was, "that's called anxiety."

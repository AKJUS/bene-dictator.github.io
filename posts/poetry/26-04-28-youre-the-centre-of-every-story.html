You're the centre of every story

Posted: 29/04/2026

Inspired by a piece of graffiti that I used to see everyday going to K1. I can no longer see it due to the construction, but I remember and that's what matters to me.

Things have felt better now than it has been for a long time! It's probably a combination of more properly feeding myself (it's what happens when you put all the money you used to spend on 91 into mince and spinach) and also actually putting myself first for a change. I still have too many things going on; I have just added another job to my long, long list of commitments; but at the moment I feel more focused, and not distracted by anybody in my life like I have been the past few years.

And, perhaps I can still find the balance between university and everything else; if you were wondering, the last piece of poetry I posted was about that... thankfully I think I might avoid that battle yet. On the other hand everyone still thinks I'm actually a political science student so?! Well, I don't fancy delaying my career even more than I already will be, and I don't even get a fancy (Hons) for my current path, just mental fortitude! I think I know which one I'd pick.

Just after I left an exec meeting last week a guy came up to me and promised me eternal life if I listened to his spiel about passover. That means all three major interpretations of Abraham has tried to proselytise me this year, which I find really funny. I have a foot in every camp and I'd rather it remain that way, it fits my personality.

This picture was taken on the Lake Daniells track when I was going to Nelson in the term break.
